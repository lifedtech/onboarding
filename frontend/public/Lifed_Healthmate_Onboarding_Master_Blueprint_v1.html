Stage 1

Pre-Qualify

A friendly explainer call to understand the host, score fit consistently, and name the first program Lifed can co-create.

What happens

  1. Schedule a 15–20 min call to explain Lifed.
  2. Score the Healthmate against 10 criteria (below).
  3. Schedule follow-ups; log internal notes.
  4. Identify the first program to co-create.

Stage 4

Review

Full validation against the curation standard. If anything needs fixing, we do it together on a call — then approve and send the SOP.

What happens

  1. Review the program with full validation.
  2. If rectification is needed, sit with the host and finish it.
  3. When ready, approve and send the conduction SOP.
  4. Whole review-to-live completes within 1 week.

Stage 5

Live

Go live, prompt sharing, send the welcome kit, and support the first bookings — stepping in if a program is quiet after ten days.

What happens

  1. Announce live; ask the host to share on their channels.
  2. Send the welcome kit — digital for all, physical for the first 100.
  3. Review the program at day 10.
  4. No booking by day 10 → trigger Sales & Marketing.
  5. Activation aims for first booking within ~3 weeks.
